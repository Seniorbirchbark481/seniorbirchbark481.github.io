⚡ kition - Organize your research and workflows

Kition helps you manage Markdown files, data tables, and AI-driven tasks in one place. Connect your research and workflows with a visual interface designed for clarity.

Download Kition for Windows

📋 Features

Kition serves as a hub for your information. You can link your Markdown notes to live data tables and use AI agents to automate research tasks. The visual workflow builder lets you map out projects without writing code.

  • Markdown Documents: Create and edit rich text files with support for images and links.
  • Data Tables: Organize information in rows and columns that sync with your notes.
  • AI Agents: Assign automated tasks to process information or find answers.
  • Browser Research: Capture web data directly into your documents.
  • Visual Workflows: Connect tasks with a drag-and-drop interface.

⚙️ System Requirements

Kition runs on standard Windows hardware. Please ensure your computer meets these conditions:

  • Operating System: Windows 10 or Windows 11.
  • Processor: 2.0 GHz or faster dual-core processor.
  • Memory: 8 GB of RAM.
  • Storage: 500 MB of space for the application files.
  • Internet Connection: Required for syncing data and AI agent functions.

📥 Installation Guide

Follow these steps to set up Kition on your Windows computer:

  1. Visit the official Kition download page.
  2. Look for the latest release version on the page.
  3. Click the file ending in .msi or .exe to download the installer to your Downloads folder.
  4. Open the downloaded file by double-clicking it.
  5. Follow the setup prompts on your screen.
  6. Launch Kition from your Start Menu after the process finishes.

🧭 How to use Kition

Once you open the app, you will see a main dashboard. This screen shows your current projects. To start, click the "New Project" button. You can add a document page to write your notes or create a table to store data. If you want to use the AI features, click the "Agent" tab to define an automation task.

Kition keeps your files locally on your computer. This ensures that you have access to your data even when you have no internet. Syncing to the cloud remains optional if you prefer to backup your work to external services.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does Kition track my personal data?
No. Kition stores all files on your local drive. We do not collect your personal notes or research data.

Can I import files from other apps?
Yes. You can import standard Markdown files and CSV data tables directly into a Kition project folder.

Is the app free to use?
Kition is an open project. You can download and use the software without fees.
